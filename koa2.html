✨ ABOUT KOLKATA ✨



🌆 Kolkata - A city full of history, culture and happiness! 🌆

Kolkata is my native place. It is one of the major cities of India and is located in West Bengal.

Kolkata is famous for its rich culture, literature, festivals, food, art and historical places.
