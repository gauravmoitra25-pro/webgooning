WELCOME TO COLLEGE An Overview The Department of Commerce, established in 1984, is the largest department of the college. Over the years, the department has evolved into a dynamic academic hub committed to excellence in commerce and management education. The department focuses on nurturing competent professionals equipped with academic knowledge, research aptitude, ethical values, and leadership qualities to meet global standards and enhance employability. Vision To impart quality education, foster creativity and research-oriented thinking, and elevate students to global standards for enhanced employability and professional excellence. Mission To provide education that not only imparts knowledge but also inculcates a sound philosophy for holistic living. To encourage self-evaluation, personality development, and guide students towards perfection and competence. To provide an excellent teaching-learning environment with a focus on continuous and lifelong education. Departmental Goals The Department strives to: Empower students with knowledge and guidance to become competent management professionals. Develop social consciousness and ethical responsibility among students. Motivate students towards holistic development. Encourage quality teaching, research, and innovation. Prepare students for leadership roles in business organizations at local, national, and international levels. Provide platforms for knowledge sharing and knowledge building to enhance career development. Programmes Offered Undergraduate Programmes (4 Year) B.Com.
